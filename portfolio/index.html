03 / The working method

The decisions are part of the work.

01

Start with the person.

A card is hard to read. A button is too easy to hit. A search fails. Turn the observed problem into a requirement the finished work can be checked against.

02

Make alternatives visible.

Ask AI for working options. Compare them at the size and in the situation where they will be used. Keep the reasons for choosing one.

03

Check before accepting.

Have another assistant inspect the work. Reproduce defects, test the correction and record what still needs a real user's playtest.

My partProblem framing, requirements, design choices, review direction and the final decision.
