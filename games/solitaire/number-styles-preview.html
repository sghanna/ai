High-Legibility Number Styles: The "6" Solution

Optimizing numeral legibility for Mom's monocular vision and eye-floater comfort on the iPhone 16e.

The Problem with standard "6": In Apple's system font, the numeral 6 has a curved neck that curls inwards towards the loop, and the negative letter-spacing (-0.5px) closes up the internal hole (counter). When viewed quickly or through an eye floater, it easily blurs into an 8, a 5, or a solid blob.

Below are 5 distinct high-legibility typographic solutions compared against the current font. Use the toggle to test legibility under a simulated eye-floater blur!

Option 1: Atkinson Hyperlegible (Braille Institute of America)

⭐ Top Recommendation for Low-Vision

Specially sculpted for visual impairment. The top stem breaks away straight at an open 55° diagonal angle with an expansive mouth (aperture), while the bottom counter is a wide, unobstructed circle. It is mathematically impossible to mistake this '6' for an 8 or 5, even through dense eye floaters.
