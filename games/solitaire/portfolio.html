The Problem Space

Commercial card games are hostile to low-vision seniors

When my mother attempted to play standard App Store solitaire games on her phone, she encountered repeated friction that led to frustration and abandonment. Commercial games optimize for ad monetization and microtransactions, neglecting accessibility fundamentals.

Deceptive Full-Screen Ads

Aggressive video popups with microscopic 10px close hitboxes tricked her into accidental App Store redirects, causing anxiety and lost game states.

Tiny Corner Indices

Standard card faces rely on thin serif fonts and miniature corner suit symbols designed for 20/20 binocular vision, blurring into grey smudges under floaters.

Accidental Restart Traps

Toolbars placed [New Game] right next to [Undo]. A fast undo tap frequently mis-tapped New Game, destroying an active winning game.

Cognitive Overload

Ticking timers, neon score animations, and flashing hints crowded the play area, creating visual noise that overwhelmed her limited field of view.

Design Leadership & AI Direction

Five core pillars where human judgment overrode AI defaults

AI coding agents naturally produce "textbook" code that fails real-world edge cases. As Product Owner, I inspected renders on real hardware, challenged automated assumptions, and established strict ergonomic rules.

01

Spatial Error Prevention (The "New Game" Isolation Rule)

AI models clustered [＋ New] and [↶ Undo] side-by-side in a 2+2 layout. Because Solitaire players spam Undo when exploring moves, proximity guarantees miss-taps.

The Override: Mandated absolute physical separation. [＋ New] is locked to the far left; [↶ Undo] is locked to the far right. Utility buttons (Menu and Help) are centered with a ~38px physical buffer zone on mobile and 48px standalone oval pills on tablet.

Design Principle
"Physical distance is superior to confirmation modals. If a destructive button is impossible to touch by accident, the player never suffers anxiety."
Result: Zero accidental game resets across hundreds of test games.
02

Character Anatomy Under Overlap (The "Q-Tail" Formula)

AI assistants squeezed tableau cascade steps to 28px or proposed arbitrary 0.48 * colWidth ratios. In Didone typography, the Queen's sole distinguishing mark is its downward diagonal tail.

The Override: In our 52×78 SVG vector card, the Queen extends to y=33. I enforced a strict mathematical floor: minUpStep = Math.max(32, Math.ceil(colWidth * 33 / 52)). At phone width (49px), the step is 32px. At iPad width (96px/129px), the step automatically expands to 61px/82px, guaranteeing the tail is never clipped.

Geometric Proof
"Typography cannot be judged in isolation. When cards stack, the exposed sliver must carry 100% of the card's semantic identity unambiguously."
Result: Queens and Tens remain instantly legible across 7-column cascades.
03

Cultural Authenticity (The Imperial Dragon Pivot)

AI models defaulted to the Double Happiness (囍) Chinese character on card backs. When testing with native Cantonese speakers, we caught that Double Happiness is exclusively a wedding motif in Hong Kong tradition and is culturally incongruous on playing cards.

The Override: Directed an exploration of 6 traditional Cantonese card motifs and selected the Imperial Golden Dragon (金龍). Scaled the medallion to an 85% card-back seal with 36 sunburst notches, a flaming ruby pearl, and 9 floating sky lanterns with auspicious blessings.

Cultural Resonance
"Accessibility includes dignity and cultural resonance. Generative AI will happily generate stereotypes unless guided by lived cultural critique."
Result: Deep emotional connection and family pride during play.
04

Peripheral Field Capture (3.4s WAAPI Descent & GPU Glow)

For a player with monocular vision and vitreal floaters, static notifications anchored at screen edges (like an "Auto-Win" button at the bottom) go completely unnoticed during active play.

The Override: Engineered a dramatic, slow 3.4-second vertical transit where the solved auto-finish banner physically descends from the very top of the display down across the tableau. Replaced choppy CSS keyframes with native Web Animations API (WAAPI) and a zero-repaint GPU compositor layer, running at 60fps/120fps.

Visual Perception
"Low-vision eyes detect broad movement across the focal field far better than static color shifts at the perimeter."
Result: 100% capture of player attention without jarring auditory alarms.
05

Multi-Device Responsive Scaling (Codex Collaboration)

Scaling from a 390pt mobile phone to a 768pt/1024pt iPad screen required more than naive CSS zoom. Tablet displays demand larger physical touch targets, deeper column scrolling, and rotation protection.

The Override: Partnered with Codex to implement 600px and 900px breakpoints, 48px tablet buttons, Deal-3 waste fanning (44px tablet step), momentum-scrolling deep tableau stacks with temporary card flight overlays, and debounced rotation safety. Verified with 12 automated unit tests.

Engineering Rigor
"Responsive design for seniors is not just scaling CSS pixels; it is scaling physical touch ergonomics and protecting active cognitive state."
Result: Flawless experience on iPhone 16e, iPad mini 5, iPad Air 2, and 11-inch iPad Pro.
