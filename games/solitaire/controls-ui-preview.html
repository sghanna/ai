agy-solitaire: 5 Control Bar & Button Options

Optimizing button placement and tap targets for one-handed iPhone 16e play and low-vision comfort.
Rendered on your locked-in Option 3 (Imperial Pine Felt & Brass) board.

Ergonomic Context for iPhone 16e: On a 6.1" display, reaching the top corners with one hand requires awkward hand shifting. Placing primary game actions (like Undo and Hint) within natural thumb reach makes a massive difference in physical comfort and prevents fingers from obstructing cards.
