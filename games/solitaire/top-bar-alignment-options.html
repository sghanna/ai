🏛️ Part 1: Top Navigation Bar Alignment Options
The top bar houses 4 key controls: New (starts a game), Menu (audio, deal modes, timer), Help (instructions), and Undo (take back move). Notice how each alignment creates a different rhythm with the 7-slot card layout below.
RECOMMENDED FOR BALANCE
Option 1: True Symmetrical Split (2 Left + 2 Right)
Natural 50/50 balance across the screen
• Left Flank (Game Flow): [＋ New] and [↶ Undo] grouped together.
• Right Flank (Utility & Guidance): [? Help] and [☰ Menu] grouped together.
• Why it works best: Creates total horizontal symmetry. Leaves the center corridor open, allowing the green felt to breathe directly over the central column (Column 3). Eliminates all awkward asymmetrical gaps.

How to Play
Klondike Solitaire Quick Guide
Goal of the Game
Build all 4 foundation piles at the top from Ace up to King, sorted by suit (♠ ♥ ♣ ♦).
Moving Cards (Tableau)
• Stack cards downward in alternating colors (e.g. Red 8 on Black 9).
• Empty column spaces can only be filled by a King.
• You can move single cards or full sequences of face-up cards.
Stock & Waste
Tap the top-right Stock pile to draw cards into the Waste pile. When empty, tap it again to recycle.
Helpful Tips for Mom
• Aces fly automatically to the foundation when tapped!
• Tap Undo anytime if you want to rethink a move.
• Try to uncover face-down cards as early as you can.
